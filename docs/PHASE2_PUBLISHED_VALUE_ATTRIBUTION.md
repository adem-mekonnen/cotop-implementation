## 1. Executive Summary & Attribution Verdict

> ### **FINAL SCIENTIFIC CLASSIFICATION: SCIENTIFICALLY UNRESOLVED (WORKLOAD AGGREGATION DISCREPANCY)**
> The published headline values ($13.90\text{ s}$, $25.14\text{ J}$) **cannot be reproduced at the individual subtask physical execution level** without violating the laws of wireless propagation and channel capacity (which bound a $2\text{ MB}$ transmission over $8.2\text{ Mbps}$ to $\approx 1.95\text{ s}$).
> 
> The evidence rigorously indicates that the published figures represent a **multi-task workload aggregation scale** (e.g., active timeslot batching or an intermediate task aggregation of $I \approx 7$ tasks) rather than individual subtask execution. Because the exact aggregation formula was not disclosed in the published manuscript, we formally record this as a **documented literature aggregation gap** rather than fitting post-hoc parameters.

## 3. Mathematical Formulae & Empirical Derivation

### `C1_PER_SUBTASK`: Per-Subtask Arithmetic Mean (A1)
- **Delay Formula**: $\text{Delay} = sum(T_k) / K = \frac{405.96 s}{200 subtasks} = 2.0298\text{ s}$
- **Energy Formula**: $\text{Energy} = sum(E_k) / K = \frac{1249.19 J}{200 subtasks} = 6.2460\text{ J}$
- **Delay Discrepancy**: Absolute = $11.8702\text{ s}$, Relative = $-85.40\%$
- **Energy Discrepancy**: Absolute = $18.8940\text{ J}$, Relative = $-75.16\%$
- **Scientific Justification**: Direct subtask physical execution (2 MB over 8.2 Mbps) is bounded by ~1.95s transmission. 13.90s is physically impossible for a single 2 MB subtask under paper-specified radio parameters.
- **Status**: **REFUTED**

---
### `C2_VEHICLE_WORKLOAD`: Vehicle-Level Full Workload Sum (I=20 Tasks)
- **Delay Formula**: $\text{Delay} = sum(T_k) / V = \frac{405.96 s}{10 vehicles} = 40.5958\text{ s}$
- **Energy Formula**: $\text{Energy} = sum(E_k) / V = \frac{1249.19 J}{10 vehicles} = 124.9195\text{ J}$
- **Delay Discrepancy**: Absolute = $26.6958\text{ s}$, Relative = $+192.06\%$
- **Energy Discrepancy**: Absolute = $99.7795\text{ J}$, Relative = $+396.90\%$
- **Scientific Justification**: Summing all 20 subtasks generated by a vehicle overshoots published values (40.6s vs 13.9s; 124.9J vs 25.1J) by ~3x to 5x.
- **Status**: **REFUTED**

---
### `C3_TIMESLOT_ACTIVE`: Active Timeslot Batch Aggregation
- **Delay Formula**: $\text{Delay} = sum(T_k) / N_active_slots = \frac{405.96 s}{29.2 active slots} = 13.9027\text{ s}$
- **Energy Formula**: $\text{Energy} = sum(E_k) / N_active_slots = \frac{1249.19 J}{29.2 active slots} = 42.7806\text{ J}$
- **Delay Discrepancy**: Absolute = $0.0027\text{ s}$, Relative = $+0.02\%$
- **Energy Discrepancy**: Absolute = $17.6406\text{ J}$, Relative = $+70.17\%$
- **Scientific Justification**: Timeslot-level execution throughput across active vehicles yields delay magnitude ~13.9s, though energy remains higher due to multi-hop transmitter RF load.
- **Status**: **PLAUSIBLE**

---
### `C4_SUB_WORKLOAD_SCALE`: Sub-Workload Batch Scale (I ≈ 6.85 subtasks)
- **Delay Formula**: $\text{Delay} = I_eff * mean(T_k) = \frac{13.90 s}{1 effective batch} = 13.9000\text{ s}$
- **Energy Formula**: $\text{Energy} = I_eff * mean(E_k) = \frac{42.77 J}{1 effective batch} = 42.7725\text{ J}$
- **Delay Discrepancy**: Absolute = $0.0000\text{ s}$, Relative = $+0.00\%$
- **Energy Discrepancy**: Absolute = $17.6325\text{ J}$, Relative = $+70.14\%$
- **Scientific Justification**: Post-hoc parameter fitting of I=6.85 matches delay exactly by construction, but lacks direct justification in the paper text which specifies I in [10, 30].
- **Status**: **UNRESOLVED**

---
### `C5_MAKESPAN_PARALLEL`: Parallel Vehicle Makespan with Standalone Energy
- **Delay Formula**: $\text{Delay} = max_{i in I}(T_i) = \frac{2.15 s (makespan)}{1 vehicle} = 2.1500\text{ s}$
- **Energy Formula**: $\text{Energy} = sum_{i in I}(E_standalone) = \frac{5.89 J}{1 vehicle} = 5.8880\text{ J}$
- **Delay Discrepancy**: Absolute = $11.7500\text{ s}$, Relative = $-84.53\%$
- **Energy Discrepancy**: Absolute = $19.2520\text{ J}$, Relative = $-76.58\%$
- **Scientific Justification**: Parallel makespan delay (2.15s) reflects parallel execution, but does not match published 13.90s.
- **Status**: **REFUTED**
