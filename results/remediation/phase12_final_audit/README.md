# Phase 12 — Final Scientific Validity, Claim Reconstruction & Publication-Readiness Audit

This directory contains the final synthesis, scientific claim reconstruction, objective performance rankings, component contribution audits, reproducibility scorecard, and publication-readiness decisions for the complete CoTOP reproduction study.

## Final Deliverables
- `REPORT.md`: Comprehensive, manuscript-level final scientific report addressing all 15 Phase 12 audit sections, publishing certification, and definitive gate verdicts.
- `final_claim_matrix.csv`: Complete claim-by-claim audit (7 core claims) with empirical evidence, statistical significance, and required qualifications.
- `objective_performance_audit.csv`: Comprehensive ranking of all 7 evaluated algorithmic variants across delay, dynamic energy, completion ratio, and collaboration rate.
- `component_contribution_audit.csv`: Mechanistic and empirical audit of GAT-GRU, Task Prioritization (Eq. 23), Multi-Head Collaboration, and A3C.
- `reproducibility_scorecard.csv`: Evaluation scorecard across 21 core scientific reproducibility dimensions.
- `paper_claims_rewritten.csv`: Defensible, publication-safe replacements for all ambiguous or scale-discrepant paper claims.
- `future_experiment_ranking.csv`: Prioritized recommendations for future experimental follow-ups.
- `manifest.json`: Machine-readable final provenance, certification, and publication-decision manifest.
- `figures/`:
  - `fig1_final_claim_distribution.png`: Distribution of claim validation outcomes.
  - `fig2_pareto_efficiency_map.png`: Multi-objective delay vs. energy trade-off map across evaluated algorithms.
  - `fig3_component_activation_summary.png`: Activation rates across all CoTOP architectural components.
  - `fig4_reproducibility_scorecard.png`: Category summary of the 21 audited reproducibility dimensions.
